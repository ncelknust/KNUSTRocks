# KNUSTRocks Research Preview

This repository contains the controlled public preview of **KNUSTRocks**, the research application developed under the **KNUST Rock Enhanced Weathering (KREW)** project and operated by the **Net-Zero Carbon Emission Lab (NCEL), KNUST**.

The preview communicates the project purpose, evidence boundaries, validation programme, institutional ownership and collaboration pathway. It intentionally excludes the complete GREW-OI analytical dataset, cell-level outputs, model-development files, uncertainty arrays, sensitivity results and unpublished manuscripts.

## Preview experience

- Responsive light and dark appearances with a persistent visitor preference;
- a complete, responsive Ghana opportunity-map visual rather than a cropped social-card image;
- a calibrated Manrope and DM Sans typography system for headings, interface text and long-form reading;
- accessible desktop and mobile navigation;
- a concise account of the research purpose, evidence architecture and staged KREW programme;
- clear separation between completed framework development and the next validation priorities;
- audience-specific pathways for scientific, field, institutional and funding collaboration; and
- a direct route for requesting a controlled technical demonstration.

## Release boundary

This public repository must not contain:

- the full KNUSTRocks or GREW-OI application;
- cell-level or district-level analytical data;
- source-variable records or mapped feedstock-distance values;
- uncertainty draws, sensitivity tables or structural-test results;
- internal code, model specifications or validation records;
- manuscripts, supporting information, peer-review records or funding documents; or
- private contact lists, credentials or unpublished partner information.

Removing a visible download control does not make browser-delivered data confidential. Only the files listed in **Public preview contents** should be deployed from this repository.

## Research status

The GREW-OI computational framework and full interactive research application are undergoing scientific, publication and institutional review. Field validation, feedstock qualification and locally appropriate MRV development remain essential next-stage activities. The preview must not be represented as evidence of verified carbon dioxide removal, project feasibility, feedstock safety, deployment readiness or carbon-credit eligibility.
